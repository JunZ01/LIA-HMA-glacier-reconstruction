# Round 190 Contract: Wang 2015 Source-Overlap Audit

## Purpose

Determine the admissible evidential role of the Wang et al. (2015) eastern
Tibetan Plateau JJA reconstruction before it is compared with any new regional
temperature operator.

## Frozen evidence

- The 12 site identities, coordinates, chronology spans, and the four sites
  declared by Wang et al. (2015) to overlap PAGES Asia2k are transcribed from
  the primary publication table and text.
- Existing project-network identity comes only from the completed Round 53,
  Round 110, Round 111, Round 149, Round 180, and Round 183 artifacts.
- Spatial distances are metadata diagnostics, not sequence-identity proof.

## Decisions

- Wang 2015 is not fully independent of the project network if any of its
  component chronologies is a current-network sequence or site overlap.
- Wang 2015 versus Hengduan remains unresolved if a Wang component is within
  15 km of a Hengduan site until raw sample identity is checked.
- Wang 2015 versus Haizi is site-independent when the nearest Wang component is
  more than 50 km away and no source identity is shared.

## Hard boundaries

- Metadata only: do not decode annual reconstruction, chronology, proxy, or
  climate values.
- Do not access or hash the prohibited Jionglacuo dataset or any alias.
- Do not access 1921--1939 numeric values.
- Do not run OOF fitting, EnKF, correction, production reconstruction, or OGGM.
