# Round 231 Contract

## Question

After adding the Yang et al. (2010) southeast-Tibet trajectory, do the four
available central/eastern-HMA records justify a uniform 1500-1899 temperature
translation of the current project field?

## Inputs

Read only locked-safe derived summaries from Rounds 206, 212, 216, 227, 229,
and 230 v2. No original climate, proxy, workbook, raster, MAT, or NetCDF value
may be opened.

## Frozen interpretation

- Historical level is assessed from the median of eight 50-year anomalies in
  1500-1899 relative to 1961-1990.
- A uniform warm correction remains rejected if all four external medians are
  positive and project median RMSE is less than half the four-family-reference
  median RMSE.
- Low-frequency window correlations are reported separately and cannot be
  converted into an additive temperature correction.
- Yang is a digitized January-June trajectory with tree-core independence but
  shared regional-investigator lineage; it is not treated as fully independent
  native numerical evidence.

## Prohibitions

No production correction, EnKF, OGGM, 1921-1939 value, or Jionglacuo access.
