# Round 238 v3 contract: NPL065 documented winter Tmin modern OOF

This contract preserves every scientific choice, threshold, and boundary from
Round 238 v1. It also preserves the v2 byte-level Tucson RWL parser.

The sole change from v2 is completion of the declared header rule: a line whose
columns 9-12 are not an integer year is skipped as descriptive metadata rather
than treated as a data-line failure. V2 was not executed. The failed v1 stopped
before ring-width or climate magnitudes were processed.

Original sources remain read-only. No 1921-1939 numeric value, paleo curve,
Jionglacuo value, EnKF, production reconstruction, correction, or OGGM run is
allowed.
