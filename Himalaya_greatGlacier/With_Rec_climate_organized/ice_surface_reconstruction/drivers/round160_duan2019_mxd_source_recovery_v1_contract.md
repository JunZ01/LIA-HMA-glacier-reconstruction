# Round 160 Duan MXD Source-Recovery Contract

## Question

Can the 17-site, 739-series southeastern Tibetan Plateau MXD network underlying
Duan et al. (2018, 2019) be recovered from anonymous public archives as raw
measurements or source chronologies?

## Allowed Evidence

- Publication text, figures, captions, references, acknowledgements, data-
  availability statements, archive catalogue pages, filenames, study metadata,
  accession identifiers, coordinates, species, investigators, and coverage years.
- HTTP response metadata and archive directory listings that contain no annual
  proxy or climate values.

## Prohibited Evidence

- Do not parse, print, summarize, or compare annual tree-ring or station values.
- Do not open or decode any 1921-1939 value.
- Do not open Jionglacuo, fit a PSM, inspect a paleo trajectory, run EnKF, or run
  OGGM.

## Decision Rule

Advance a source only if an anonymous public URL or accession exists and
publication/archive metadata directly identifies it as one of the 17 network
sites or as the published 739-series regional MXD source. Similar location,
species, investigator, or title alone is insufficient. If site identities or
public files cannot be established, close public recovery and record the exact
missing item instead of guessing from the map.
