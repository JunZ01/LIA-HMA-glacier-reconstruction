# Proxy9 Grouped Calibration-Window Attribution v1

This no-OGGM experiment attributes the previously observed response to moving
the full proxy9 PSM network from the archived 1940-2000 calibration window to
1920-1980.

The 297 records are partitioned into mutually exclusive groups using archived
HMA contribution and calibration-window operator instability. The only changed
quantity in each branch is the declared group's delta-anchored 1920-1980 PSM
operator. Proxy observations, all other operators, prior, sample indices,
localization, archived RegEM-completed diagonal error variances, model type,
seasonality, target extraction, HMA mask, and instrumental products are fixed.

The duplicate chronology pair proxy9 records 291 and 296 is isolated before
the remaining records are split at the nonduplicate-network 75th percentiles
of impact and instability. The lower-instability label is relative to this
network threshold and is not a claim of physical stability.

`rec_ref` remains comparison-only. No group is accepted, deleted, or promoted
to production by this attribution experiment.
