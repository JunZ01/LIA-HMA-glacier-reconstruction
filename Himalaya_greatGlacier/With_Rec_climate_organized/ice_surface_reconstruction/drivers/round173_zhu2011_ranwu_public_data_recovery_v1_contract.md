# Round 173 Zhu 2011 Ranwu Public-Data Recovery Contract

## Question

Can the raw Ranwu Balfour-spruce TRW measurements, RCS chronology, or annual
August-temperature reconstruction underlying Zhu et al. (2011), DOI
`10.1016/j.palaeo.2011.02.017`, be recovered anonymously from an official
publisher attachment or established data repository?

## Allowed Search

- Official Elsevier attachment paths for PII `S003101821100085X`.
- Crossref, DataCite, Zenodo, and Figshare metadata APIs.
- NOAA metadata evidence already closed in Rounds 171-172.

## Protection

- Candidate attachments may be saved byte-for-byte under `raw_unopened`.
- No downloaded candidate file is parsed, rendered, OCR-processed, or opened.
- No annual proxy, reconstruction, climate, 1921-1939, or Jionglacuo value is
  accessed.

## Decision

- Advance only when a public attachment or repository record plausibly exposes
  raw TRW, chronology, or annual reconstruction data.
- Otherwise close anonymous public recovery and prepare an author/archive
  request that also asks whether Huang 2025 reuses the Zhu 2011 cores.

No EnKF, OGGM, or production reconstruction is authorized.
