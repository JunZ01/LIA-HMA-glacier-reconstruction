## 303. Qamdo 2 raw-chronology identity and modern-OOF closure (2026-08-21)

- Round 167 recovered the official CHIN074/Qamdo 2 raw ring-width archive without registration. It contains 144 samples spanning allowed years 128-2010. CHIN074 shares no exact or normalized sample identifier with the older CHIN046/CHIN047 Qamdo records, and its derived standard chronology has zero exact-affine or near-exact sequence aliases across all 202 proxy6 and 297 proxy9 records.
- The raw Tucson measurements were detrended once with the prospectively frozen `dplPy 0.1.5` modified-negative-exponential residual method and combined using a Tukey biweight chronology without prewhitening. Modern sample depth is 59 in 1940 and remains at least 27 through 2000. The derived chronology correlates `0.6464` with the published Qamdo annual-temperature reconstruction over 1940-2000; the published reconstruction was used only for lineage/orientation and never as an OOF predictor.
- Five-block previous-calendar-year annual-temperature OOF testing gives Berkeley Earth correlation/skill `0.0739/-0.0575` and CRU `0.2794/+0.1042`. Both products have positive fold correlations in all five blocks, all ten fitted slopes are positive, and both early/late split correlations are positive, but neither product reaches the required `r >= 0.30` joint gate and Berkeley has negative skill.
- Decision: close Qamdo 2 as a standalone transferable temperature operator. It is genuinely new fitting evidence and carries a directionally stable local temperature response, but it does not provide enough cross-product modern OOF skill to justify a signal-free RCS replication, paleo-trajectory inspection, production reconstruction, or bias correction. Do not relax the frozen gate or tune season, lag, chronology method, site, or sign from this result.
