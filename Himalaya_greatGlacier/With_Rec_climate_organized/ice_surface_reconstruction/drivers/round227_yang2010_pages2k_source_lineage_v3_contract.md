# Round 227 v3: source-specific metadata closure

Round 227 v2 stopped because the migrated CHIN045 data.gov page omitted its
coverage years. This version keeps that failed output untouched and assigns each
field to its authoritative source: PAGES2k Supplementary Table 1 supplies ID,
DOI, coordinates, and coverage; data.gov supplies archive investigator, site,
ITRDB code, and species; FAU supplies Yang 2010 authors and candidate metadata.

Only metadata may be read. Raw tree-ring measurements, chronologies, annual
reconstruction values, 1921-1939 values, Jionglacuo, production climate, EnKF,
MATLAB, and OGGM remain forbidden. No existing result may be overwritten.
