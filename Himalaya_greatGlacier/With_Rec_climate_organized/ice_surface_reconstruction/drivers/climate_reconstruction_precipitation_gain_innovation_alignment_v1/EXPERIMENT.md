# Precipitation Gain-Innovation Alignment V1

Date: 2026-08-13

## Purpose

Separate widespread proxy innovation chronology from the identity-specific
mapping imposed by the frozen prior-to-HMA Kalman gain.

The experiment uses the completed proxy6 EnKF experiment-1 attribution for
1901-1920. It preserves all 202 innovation trends and all 202 HMA gain weights,
but permutes their proxy-identity pairing. A second null permutes only within
four longitude bands, preserving broad regional composition.

This is a read-only attribution diagnostic. It does not change or select a
proxy, PSM, prior, covariance, EnKF parameter, or reconstruction method. The
locked 1921-1939 period remains unaccessed.
