# LIA Project Facts Addendum 356

## 20CRv3 exclusion from temperature validation (2026-08-26)

- The user explicitly prohibits use of 20CRv3 for the HMA temperature
  reconstruction diagnosis and correction mainline.
- Do not download new 20CRv3 values, open or analyze historical 20CRv3 values,
  cite 20CRv3 as validation evidence, or use it to select any temperature
  correction. Existing versioned 20CRv3 outputs remain preserved as historical
  artifacts but are excluded from current and future scientific decisions.
- The prohibition does not authorize substituting another reanalysis without
  its own independence, regional-skill, and uncertainty audit.
- The 2026-08-26 route check read only NOAA public documentation and downloaded
  no new 20CRv3 data or 1850-1950 values.
