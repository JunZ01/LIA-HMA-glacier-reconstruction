# Proxy9 Grouped Calibration-Window Attribution v2

This corrected no-OGGM experiment attributes the response to moving proxy9
PSM calibration operators from 1940-2000 to 1920-1980.

The 297 records are partitioned into mutually exclusive groups using archived
HMA contribution and calibration-window operator instability. Each isolated
branch changes only one declared group's delta-anchored operator. Proxy
observations, all other operators, prior, samples, localization, archived
RegEM-completed diagonal error variances, model type, seasonality, HMA mask,
and instrumental comparisons remain frozen.

Records 291 and 296 are isolated as the only duplicated combined chronology.
The other records are split at the nonduplicate-network 75th percentiles of
impact and instability. `rec_ref` remains comparison-only. This experiment
does not authorize record deletion, coefficient constraints, or adoption of a
production calibration design.
