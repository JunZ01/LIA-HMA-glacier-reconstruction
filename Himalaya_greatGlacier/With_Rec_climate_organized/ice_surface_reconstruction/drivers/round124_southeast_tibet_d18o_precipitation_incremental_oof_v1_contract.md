# Round 124 Southeast Tibet d18O Precipitation Incremental OOF

## Objective

Test whether the independently qualified MiMei and Xinpu tree-ring d18O series
improve the frozen Round 117 annual precipitation residual reconstruction.

## Frozen design

- Control: the exact 25-proxy Round 117 documented-moisture branch.
- Candidate branches: control plus MiMei, control plus Xinpu, and control plus both.
- Years: 1940-2000 only.
- Outer blocks, inner rank/ridge selection, climate products, HMA grid support,
  legacy reconstruction, scoring metrics, and eleven modern gates are unchanged.
- Candidate values for 1921-1939 are not decoded.
- The sealed final-test reserve is not opened.
- Original files are read-only and no OGGM experiment is run.

## Decision

The two-record branch advances only if it passes every frozen modern gate.
Individual metric changes are reported even if the full branch fails, so a
useful spatial signal is not confused with validation of the complete method.
