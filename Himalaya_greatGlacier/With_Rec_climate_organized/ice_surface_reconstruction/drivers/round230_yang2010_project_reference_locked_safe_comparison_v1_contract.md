# Round 230 Contract

## Question

Does the independent Yang et al. (2010) southeast-Tibet January-June
temperature trajectory support a persistent 1500-1899 warm bias in the current
local project reconstruction, and is the project trajectory closer to this
record than the four-family reference trajectory?

## Frozen design

- Site: 29.3666667 N, 94.2666667 E.
- Spatial statistic: area-weighted 2 x 2 degree box centered on the site.
- Project temperature: the frozen six-member project mean.
- Reference temperature: the frozen four-family reference field.
- Common temporal scale: centered 11-year mean for project and reference;
  Yang uses the digitized published 11-year FFT low-pass curve.
- Baseline: 1961-1990 for every curve.
- Historical diagnostic: eight non-overlapping 50-year windows, 1500-1899.
- Common visible segments: 1377-1899 and 1940-1998 only.
- The entire 1900-1939 interval is excluded from the comparison. No numeric
  value in the sealed 1921-1939 interval may be read.

## Frozen decision gate

Yang supports a persistent local project warm-bias direction only if at least
six of eight Yang historical windows are below zero and the median project
minus Yang anomaly is at least +0.15 degC. This local, seasonal, digitized
record cannot authorize an HMA-wide correction or a production rerun by itself.

## Prohibitions

- No interpolation or inference in 1900-1939.
- No Jionglacuo access.
- No production climate, EnKF, OGGM, source MAT, TIFF, NetCDF, CSV, or workbook
  modification.
