# Round 227 v2: corrected identity and source-lineage decision

This version supersedes only the identifier matching and lineage decision in
Round 227 v1. The official workbook is reused byte-for-byte.

Allowed inputs are the Round 227 v1 metadata workbook, Round 226 official FAU
item metadata and summary, and the two official data.gov metadata pages for
ITRDB CHIN044 and CHIN045. These sources contain metadata only.

The audit may compare identifiers, DOI, author names, species labels, site
coordinates, and record coverage. It must not download or open raw ring-width
files, chronologies, annual reconstructions, figures, PAGES2k LiPD/CSV/MAT
values, 1921-1939 values, Jionglacuo material, EnKF files, production climate,
MATLAB, or OGGM. Existing files and result directories must not be overwritten.
