# Proxy9 Calibration-Window EnKF v1

This controlled experiment tests whether PSM calibration-window position can
explain the early-20th-century HMA temperature bias.

The two alternative operator branches use common-random-number delta anchors
relative to the fresh 1940-2000 replay. Proxy observations, prior, sample
indices, archived RegEM-completed diagonal error variances, annual seasonality,
PSM model type, HMA mask, and instrumental comparisons are frozen. No
coefficient sign is constrained and no proxy is removed.

`rec_ref` is plotted only as comparison evidence. Acceptance must be judged
against multiple instrumental products and hard numerical closure.
