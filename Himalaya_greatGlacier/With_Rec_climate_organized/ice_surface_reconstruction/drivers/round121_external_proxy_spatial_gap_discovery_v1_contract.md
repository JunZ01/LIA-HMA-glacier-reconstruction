# Round 121 External Proxy Spatial-Gap Discovery Contract

## Objective

Find genuinely new, public proxy evidence that can address the remaining HMA
temperature spatial-skill failure or precipitation-amplitude uncertainty. The
primary target is plateau-interior and east-central HMA evidence, not another
near-duplicate southeastern chronology.

## Search And Triage Rules

- Search primary public repositories and publication data archives first:
  NOAA/WDS Paleoclimatology, publisher supplements, Figshare, Zenodo, Mendeley
  Data, PANGAEA, and the National Tibetan Plateau Data Center when anonymous
  download is available.
- Prefer raw measurements or published standardized chronologies with annual or
  seasonal climate interpretation and coverage extending through the modern
  blocked-calibration period.
- Regional reconstructed climate series may be acquired as external benchmarks,
  but are not fitting proxies unless their underlying chronology and defensible
  operator/error information are separately available.
- Rank candidates by location relative to current-network gaps, low-frequency
  preservation, chronology length, calibration metadata, public access, and
  material/site distinctness from the current 311-record pool.
- Download into this round's independent `raw_unopened` directory. Do not modify
  any existing source or production proxy file.
- During discovery, inspect repository and publication metadata only. Do not
  analyze candidate values and do not decode, compare, retain, or output any
  1921-1939 candidate value.
- The frozen external validation reserve remains sealed and OGGM remains
  prohibited.
