# LIA-RGI transient inversion: constraint inventory

**Document version:** v1 frozen data-decision baseline (2026-07-19)

The schema, causal rules, and five product decisions in this version are
frozen. The remaining open gates are listed in Section 8 and must be resolved
in the Stage 0/1 implementation specification before calibration starts.

## 1. Purpose and scope

This document maps every required model constraint to the observation that is
allowed to identify it. The governing rule is:

> Identifiability must close within each physical layer. Information may pass
> from a faster layer to a slower layer, but a slow-layer residual must never
> rewrite a fast-layer parameter.

This is the Windows decision baseline for the organized workspace. Original
notebooks and original OGGM working directories remain read-only. New data,
registries, temporary files, and outputs for this baseline are restricted to
the organized `E:` workspace.

Status values:

- `confirmed-local`: the data asset is present in this repository.
- `confirmed-decision`: the product/method is frozen; acquisition is deferred to its owning stage.
- `external-reference`: code contains a path, but the asset is not in the repository.
- `selected-candidate`: the product policy is selected, but the exact version/path is not yet registered locally.
- `missing-decision`: a dataset or uncertainty model still has to be selected.
- `confirmed-structural-scenario`: non-identifiability is represented by explicit scenarios rather than a fitted point value.
- `model-product`: generated by OGGM or another model and not an independent observation.

## 2. Non-negotiable calibration boundaries

`Layer` names the physical time-scale hierarchy. `Stage` is reserved for the
six-stage workflow, so code, artifacts, and documents cannot confuse the two.

| Physical layer | Workflow owner | Identified quantity | Allowed evidence | Forbidden feedback |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Layer 0, instantaneous dynamics | Stage 1 static calibration, under a Stage 0 structural scenario | Effective rheology (`glen_a`) within a declared thickness-product and sliding scenario | Modern thickness/volume product; sparse direct thickness only where available | LIA reachability, modern endpoint residual, or reusing velocity as a second likelihood term |
| Layer 1, annual surface balance | Stage 1 static MB calibration and Stage 2 ensemble freeze | `prcp_fac`, `melt_f`, `temp_bias` | Modern geodetic MB and precipitation prior; modern velocity retained as soft structural evidence | LIA geometry, `SP/LIA`, `FW/RGI`, or velocity-weighted parameter contraction |
| Layer 2, centennial geometry | Stage 3 initial-state search | Initial model state only | LIA geometry plus dating and geometry uncertainty | Rewriting Stage 1/2 parameter artifacts |
| Transient validation | Stage 4 forward run | No calibration parameter | Reconstructed climate as forcing; modern RGI as terminal validation | Any write to physical parameter artifacts |
| Diagnosis and outer loops | Stage 5 diagnosis | Climate correction or structural-scenario decision | Residual sign, uncertainty, and stratification | Hiding residuals in faster-layer parameters |

The Stage 1 apparent-MB consistency loop may recompute Layer 0/1 parameters
using modern same-layer observations only. Stage 2 freezes representative
points along the modern-MB equivalence surface without velocity weighting.
Velocity remains registered as soft structural evidence, a routing signal, and
a Stage 4 residual-interpretation aid. Neither stage may ingest LIA or
forward-run metrics.

### 2.1 Observation reuse policy

One observation error realization contributes to the likelihood only once.
The conservative v1 policy is:

- Stage 1 uses thickness/volume products to identify effective `glen_a`; it does
  not use surface velocity to tighten the Layer 0 posterior.
- Stage 2 does not use velocity to contract or select the Layer 1 equivalence
  surface. It registers the velocity diagnostic once as soft evidence and
  exports the unsqueezed equivalence ensemble to Stage 3.
- A future joint Layer 0/1 likelihood may replace this soft-evidence policy only
  as a new registered method version. It must not duplicate the retained
  velocity evidence and must carry the full cross-parameter covariance.

Millan thickness is itself velocity-driven. It would therefore not be
statistically independent of any future surface-velocity likelihood even when
the files differ. That
dependence must be declared in the data provenance, represented by covariance
or conservative uncertainty inflation, and exposed through the thickness-product
structural axis below.

## 4. Assets that must not be counted as observations

The following values occur in current notebooks but are simulation outputs:

- `model_diagnostics.volume_m3`
- `model_diagnostics.area_m2`
- `model_diagnostics.length_m`
- OGGM `distributed_thickness`
- the generated `Himalaya_bare_DEM.tif`
- spinup and forward-run `SP/LIA` and `FW/RGI` ratios

They are valid diagnostics or model state variables. They are not independent
constraints for `glen_a`, sliding, or MB parameters unless compared with a
separately versioned observational product.

## 5. Structural scenarios to expose explicitly

At minimum, the first structural ensemble should contain:

| Axis | Scenario A | Scenario B | Reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ice motion partition | `fs=0`, all effective motion assigned to deformation | Sliding enabled with an explicit `fs` prior | Modern velocity identifies total flux more directly than the deformation/sliding split |
| Debris process | No debris correction | Debris-aware ablation for mapped debris glaciers | Prevent debris-related residuals from being absorbed by `melt_f` |
| Bed/flowline geometry | Current OGGM/bare-DEM construction | Declared alternative bed or flowline configuration | Make geometry extrapolation error a beta-loop quantity |
| Thickness target product | Farinotti 2019 consensus | Millan 2022 thickness, with GlaThiDa checks where available | No full-coverage product is direct measurement; HMA product differences can reach 30-50%, so effective `glen_a` is conditional on product choice |

Each scenario is a discrete structure version. Stage 4 produces terminal
residuals, and Stage 5 may use them to select or weight scenarios, but neither
stage may silently rewrite Stage 1/2 continuous parameter artifacts.

## 6. Parameter provenance stamp

Every parameter row must carry at least these fields:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `glacier_id` | Stable RGI or LIA identifier |
| `parameter` / `value` / `unit` | Parameter payload |
| `physical_layer` | `instantaneous`, `annual_mb`, or `centennial_state` |
| `written_by_stage` | Only the owning workflow stage may write the value |
| `observation_ids` | Exact observations used to identify it |
| `observation_use_id` | Stable identifier proving that one observation likelihood was counted only once |
| `data_version_hash` | Hash/version of every observation input |
| `method_version` / `code_hash` | Calibration implementation provenance |
| `run_id` / `parent_run_id` | Re-entry and lineage |
| `prior`, `bounds` | Prior distribution and admissible interval |
| `posterior_mean`, `posterior_std` | Point summary plus uncertainty; never point value alone |
| `convergence_metrics` | Residuals and rank/conditioning diagnostics |
| `status` / `failure_code` | Machine-readable outcome |
| `created_at` | UTC timestamp |

Stage 3 or later must never write Layer 0/1 parameter artifacts. Stage 2 may
write only the declared immutable freeze of the unsqueezed Layer 1 equivalence
ensemble and its routing evidence. Any other cross-stage write must fail
validation.

## 7. Required quality checks before implementation

1. Quantify the dependence of each thickness product on model assumptions and
   velocity inputs. Do not require impossible full-coverage measurement
   independence; instead, report product-conditional effective rheology and
   marginalize over the declared thickness/sliding structural scenarios.
2. Build the whitened, dimensionless Layer 1 sensitivity/Jacobian matrix for the
   actual three-observation set and report its numerical rank and condition
   number per glacier. Pre-register the singular-value tolerance and
   `kappa_max` in the Stage 1/2 implementation specification.
3. Define spatially varying observation errors instead of forcing one global
   confidence level, especially for Karakoram precipitation and low-speed/debris glaciers.
4. Define LIA comparison metrics before any spinup: area, main-flowline length,
   hypsometry, and their observation covariance.
5. Version every structural input: RGI/LIA crosswalk, DEM, bed, flowline type,
   debris scenario, and sliding scenario.
6. Preserve the historical cross-layer scans as a baseline experiment only;
   never import their selected `melt_f` or `temp_bias` into disciplined parameter artifacts.

### 7.1 Weak-identifiability decision rule

If the whitened Jacobian is numerically rank-deficient or its condition number
exceeds the pre-registered `kappa_max`, the glacier is not forced to a point
estimate. It must:

- receive `status = weak-identifiability` plus a machine-readable failure code;
- retain a widened or explicitly prior-dominated MB posterior interval;
- export interval/ensemble draws, not a single best parameter row, to later stages;
- remain in the workflow with reduced precision, with that uncertainty propagated
  into the climate-inference C line.

This is reduced precision, not workflow exclusion. Complete data/model failure
remains a separate classified failure state.

## 8. Data-decision closure and implementation gates

The five product decisions are closed for inventory v1:

1. Thickness: Farinotti 2019 primary and Millan 2022 v1.1 structural scenario,
   documented in `02_thickness_data_decision.md`.
2. Velocity: ITS_LIVE v2.1 primary turnover observation with a frozen gate
   operator and no Stage 1 reuse, documented in `03_velocity_data_decision.md`.
3. Precipitation and baseline climate: GSWP3_W5E5 2023.2 primary with a widened
   Upper Indus/Karakoram prior, documented in `04_precipitation_climate_data_decision.md`.
4. LIA timing: four regional trapezoid windows with glacier-specific timing
   latent, documented in `05_lia_timing_data_decision.md`.
5. Debris: Scherler LS8_RATIO primary, FDC algorithm scenario, and Herreid 2020
   comparison, documented in `06_debris_data_decision.md`.

Inventory v1 is frozen as a data-decision artifact. This does not authorize a
simulation run. The Stage 0/1 implementation specification must still close:

- exact DEM, modern geodetic-MB, and reconstructed-climate paths, versions,
  hashes, units, and uncertainty fields;
- LIA and modern-geometry covariance and comparison metrics;
- the whitened Jacobian singular-value tolerance and `kappa_max`;
- the enabled-sliding scenario prior and source stamp;
- validation hooks enforcing stage ownership and posterior widening.

Until those implementation gates are registered, the workflow remains in
design/audit mode and must not start another centennial parameter scan.
